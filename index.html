The limits LogChecker audits against, and how it counts time.

Method

How time is counted

Work and rest are recorded in 15-minute blocks, the same unit as the national work diary.

Periods shorter than 24 hours are counted forward from the end of every rest break. Periods of 24 hours or more are counted forward from the end of a relevant major rest break of 7 continuous hours, and from the first record.

A night rest break is 7 continuous hours of stationary rest between 10pm and 8am, or a 24-hour continuous rest break.

When overlapping periods report the same event, LogChecker keeps one breach per event, at the time the limit was first passed and at the largest amount over.

Days with no record count as rest. Breach risk categories follow the HVNL minor, substantial, severe and critical bands. The night rest categories are LogChecker's own banding.

LogChecker is an audit aid. Confirm every finding against the driver's work diary and the current Heavy Vehicle National Law and Heavy Vehicle (Fatigue Management) National Regulation before taking action. Advanced Fatigue Management (AFM) accreditations have operator-specific limits and are not audited here.
